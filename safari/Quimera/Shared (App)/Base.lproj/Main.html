You can turn on Quimera’s Safari extension in Settings.

Here's how to access Quimera in Safari:

You can turn on Quimera’s extension in Safari Extensions preferences.

Quimera’s extension is currently on. You can turn it off in Safari Extensions preferences.

Quimera’s extension is currently off. You can turn it on in Safari Extensions preferences.

Due to some limitation on the Safari browser, not all cookies can be displayed. For example, Safari can't display cookies with "HTTPOnly" set to true.
